# Other Prospective Collaborators (exploratory)

Per Sanjay, Aug 2026 ([Slack](https://fplaunchpad.slack.com/archives/C0B3GBWL97U/p1787040269883279)): the group is "not married to" CoreStack and TESSERA. These are other environment/public-tech collaborators surfaced so far, not yet tied to a project-management issue.

## Candidates

1. [PDGI](https://pdgi.org) (Kaustubh Maske Patil's contact): collective started by FOSS United, Rainmatter, SAFAR India, Pranava Institute, Tech4Good and others, re-imagining public tech to be built with people's participation from the design stage. Climate/ecology is one of their axes alongside commons-building. Reference framework: [Bhilwara Principles](https://safarindia.org/bhilwara-principles).
2. [SAFAR India](https://safarindia.org) street-vendor-union digital platform pilot (Kaustubh, in progress): building a management platform for a street vendors' union in Meghalaya, with a longer-term goal of the state government adopting the same platform for surveying/licensing/welfare, and potential absorption into PDGI.
3. [IIT Bombay Climate Studies group](https://www.climate.iitb.ac.in/) (surfaced by Alina): example recent work — [Mumbai Flood](https://www.mumbaiflood.in/about).
4. Frappe-like public-tech platform (idea from Kaustubh, unstaffed): a generic, extendable no-code platform (schemas, websites, databases) for public-tech use cases, positioned as a more robust alternative to [Frappe Framework](https://frappe.io/framework).
